🛂 USCIS U.S. Immigration Reality Check: EB-2 NIW, Matter of Shah / EDGE & Foreign Prestige

1. The EB-2 Advanced Degree Trap (8 CFR § 204.5(k)(2))

• Strict Statutory Rule: Qualifying via Advanced Degree requires a U.S. Master's (or foreign equivalent), OR a 4-year Bachelor's + 5 years progressive post-bachelor experience.
• Matter of Shah & AACRAO EDGE: USCIS presumes a 4-year undergraduate foundation and is not bound by WES evaluations.
• Brazil Credential Map (WES): 2-yr Tecnólogo (1,748h) = U.S. Associate's (not Bachelor's); Pós-Graduação Lato Sensu = Graduate Certificate (not Master's). Only Mestrado Stricto Sensu w/ dissertação equals a U.S. Master's.
• ⚠️ RFE Risk: Stacking a 1-year Spanish/UK Master's directly over a 2-year Tecnólogo risks an EB-2 RFE or direct denial for missing the 4-year undergrad baseline.
2. Two Clean Solutions + University Recognition Before USCIS

• Path A — U.S. 4-Yr B.S. Bridge First (UMPI / TESU / Excelsior): In ~5–10 months you hold a real regionally accredited U.S. 4-yr B.S. (zero EDGE friction, unlocks any U.S. M.S., and Excelsior adds NSA/DHS CAE-CD weight).
• Path B — EB-2 Exceptional Ability (8 CFR § 204.5(k)(3)(ii)): Bypass the Master's gate by proving at least 3 of 6 criteria (10+ yrs full-time experience, academic credentials, professional certs/licenses, high salary, associations, peer/gov recognition) + Matter of Dhanasar 3 prongs.
• "Renowned Name" (Dhanasar Prong 2): UK chartered universities (Liverpool, UoL / Royal Holloway, Open University) hold stronger U.S./global recognition than private Spanish online brands (UNIR / VIU) — though USCIS decides Prong 2 mainly on real impact (13.5y PMMG chain of custody, 4y consular PII compliance, 14 AI repos), not university brand alone.
